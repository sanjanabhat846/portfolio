Project Detail

NutriScan AI

A computer vision application that recognizes food items and estimates nutritional information to support healthier eating decisions.

Overview

NutriScan AI helps users understand food nutrition by analyzing images and predicting labels and approximate nutritional values.

Problem Statement

Many people struggle to estimate calories and nutrition manually, especially when eating out or managing diet goals.

Solution

Users upload food images, and the AI predicts the food category along with estimated calories, protein, carbohydrates, and fats.

Features

Image recognition, nutrition estimation, user-friendly UI, and practical recommendations for healthier choices.

Technologies Used

Python, TensorFlow, Keras, OpenCV, Flask, HTML, CSS, JavaScript.

Challenges

Creating accurate predictions from diverse food categories and handling differences in portion sizes and presentation.

Future Improvements

Barcode scanning, meal planning, diet recommendations, and mobile accessibility.
