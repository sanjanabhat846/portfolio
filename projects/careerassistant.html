Project Detail

AI Career Assistant

A generative AI-powered career guidance platform that helps users explore suitable career paths and improve job readiness.

Overview

This project combines AI and user profiling to provide personalized career advice based on a person’s background and interests.

Problem Statement

Many students and job seekers struggle to identify the best career options and improve resumes without guidance.

Solution

The assistant analyzes resumes, skills, and interests to offer guidance around career direction and suitable opportunities.

Features

Resume analysis, skill gap detection, AI recommendations, job matching, and personalized guidance.

Technologies Used

Python, Flask, Machine Learning, NLP, HTML, CSS, JavaScript.

Challenges

Balancing personalization with general usability and making recommendations actionable and meaningful.

Future Improvements

Interview preparation support, real-time job alerts, chatbot assistance, and deeper profile analysis.
