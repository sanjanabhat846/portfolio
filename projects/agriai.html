🌱 AgriSense AI

AgriSense AI is an AI-powered crop disease detection system designed to help farmers quickly identify plant diseases from leaf images. It uses Deep Learning and Computer Vision to classify healthy and diseased crops, enabling early intervention and reducing crop losses.

Problem Statement

Farmers often struggle to identify crop diseases at an early stage, leading to reduced yields and financial losses. Manual diagnosis requires agricultural expertise, which may not always be accessible.

Solution

AgriSense AI provides a simple interface where users upload a leaf image. A trained Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) predicts whether the leaf is healthy or diseased and displays the prediction instantly.

Features

  • Leaf image upload
  • AI disease prediction
  • Deep Learning CNN model
  • Fast image preprocessing
  • User-friendly interface
  • Real-time prediction
